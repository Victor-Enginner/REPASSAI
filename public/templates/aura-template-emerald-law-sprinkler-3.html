Intelligent Systems For Modern Landscapes

We utilize the latest in water conservation and smart-home technology to ensure your lawn stays lush while reducing waste and lowering bills.

Rain Sensors

  • Saves water by 30-35% over basic irrigation controllers.
  • Prevents over watering during or after sufficient rainfall.
  • Convenient bypass features included.

WiFi Clocks

  • Control your system from anywhere in the world.
  • Controller receives data from local weather stations and automatically adjusts to current conditions.
  • Sends alerts if the system is not functioning properly, or monitor broken lines (flow sensor required).
  • Have ELS manage your system remotely from our office.
Compliance & Safety

Backflow Testing

Recommended highly by the DEP every year on the opening of your irrigation system. Done to protect any back siphonage from chemicals, toxins or pesticides into your drinking water. SHOULD BE DONE EVERY YEAR.
